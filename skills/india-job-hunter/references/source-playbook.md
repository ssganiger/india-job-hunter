# India job search source playbook

Use this for broad live sweeps. These are examples to adapt, not a required checklist. Check that each site and listing is current before relying on it.

## Search order

1. Employer career and public ATS pages for likely target companies. Use the [target-company seed](target-companies.md) only to shortlist relevant candidates, then add employers found elsewhere. Search the company site and the exact title or job ID.
2. Broad portals and aggregators such as LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri, Foundit, Indeed, and Google Jobs. Use these to discover roles, then seek the employer listing.
3. Relevant niche boards, recruiter posts, and public professional communities. Choose by seniority and domain; early-career boards may be irrelevant to senior candidates.

## Query patterns

- `"<title variant>" "<city or India>" jobs`
- `"<company>" "<title>" careers India`
- `"<job ID>" "<company>"`
- `site:<company domain> careers "<skill>"`
- `"<domain>" "<two distinctive skills>" hiring India`
- `site:jobs.lever.co "<company>" "<title>"`
- `site:jobs.ashbyhq.com "<company>" "<title>"`
- `site:myworkdayjobs.com "<company>" "<title>"`

Other common public ATS hosts include Greenhouse and SmartRecruiters. URL patterns can change; discover the current employer board instead of constructing an assumed apply URL.

## Coverage notes

Record source type, useful query or target, result count if known, and whether a direct listing was opened. Retry a blocked listing with a job ID or employer search when the role is promising. Stop low-yield repeats and report what could not be verified. A company expansion article is context, not a job listing.
