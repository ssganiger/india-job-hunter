# Target companies seed list (India)

Source: user-supplied list, incorporated 2026-10-01. Entries have not been individually checked for current name, India presence, careers-page availability, or open roles. Some entries are brands, groups, divisions, agencies, public bodies, or cities rather than distinct employers. Treat all entries as discovery candidates, not evidence of hiring.

**This is a starting list, not a limit and not exhaustive.** India's employer landscape changes constantly through mergers, rebrands, new centers, and closures. Use it to seed a profile-relevant career-page sweep, then:

1. Keep only the companies relevant to the user's profile (domain, seniority, skills, locations).
2. Add companies discovered through search: competitors, vendors, customers, newly funded firms, newly announced India centers.
3. Verify each company still exists and is hiring in India before relying on it. Mergers and rebrands are common (e.g. Mindtree is now part of LTIMindtree; Zomato's parent is Eternal).

## 10. Ways to discover more

- Industry lists: NASSCOM reports, Zinnov GCC landscape, Great Place to Work India lists, Naukri and LinkedIn "top companies" lists, company-size filters on LinkedIn
- Competitors, vendors and customers of the companies already matching the user's profile
- Alumni and ex-colleague employers on LinkedIn
